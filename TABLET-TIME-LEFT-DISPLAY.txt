GUSTAVO'S COINTAB DASHBOARD - TABLET TIME LEFT

Added to every tablet card:
- Standalone / Centralized / Smart modes:
  Shows TIME LEFT as HH:MM:SS while remainingSeconds > 0.
- Direct USB Charger Only:
  Shows MINUTES LEFT as whole remaining minutes while remainingSeconds > 0.
- When remainingSeconds is 0:
  The entire time/minutes display is hidden. No 0 MIN and no 00:00:00.

Existing Seller Transfer feature is preserved.
